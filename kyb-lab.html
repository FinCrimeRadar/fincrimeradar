KYB Lab

KYB Lab reads one UK company's public Companies House record and sets out what the register says, with each fact, date and gap labelled as such. It is not customer due diligence, and it does not produce a risk score or a verdict.

2 to 160 characters. This searches company names only, not people.

Companies returned

    For example 00445790 or SC123456. Short numeric numbers are padded with zeros.

    What this readout does not establish

      Source: the Companies House public register, read through the Companies House Public Data API. This page searches company names only. Searching for a person or an officer is not supported.
