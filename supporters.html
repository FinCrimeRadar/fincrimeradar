FinCrimeRadar is free and will stay free. This page explains how it's funded today and how to support it directly if you find it useful.

How FinCrimeRadar is funded

FinCrimeRadar is funded through display advertising on a small number of practitioner guide pages, and, in future, affiliate relationships with industry training and certification providers alongside a planned freemium API tier. See the Editorial Standards page for how that funding is kept separate from editorial content: no paid placement influences which topics get covered or what a guide concludes.

Sponsor FinCrimeRadar

Readers and organisations who find the screening tool or the guides useful can also support the project directly through GitHub Sponsors. Sponsorship helps cover hosting, data, and the time that goes into keeping the guides current as regulation changes.

Every contribution helps keep the tool free and independent.
