# The De-Risking Judgement Call: external review brief (CLAUDE.md section 7)

For a manual ChatGPT or Codex review outside Claude Code. One page. No prose has been drafted for the guide, so you are reviewing claims and readings, not copy.

**Instruction to the reviewer.** Test each claim against the primary text, not against our source pack. The pack (`docs/review-queue/de-risking-judgement-call-source-pack.md`) records what we read and what we concluded. Treat its conclusions as hypotheses to break. Fetch the primary provision yourself, quote the operative words, and say where you disagree, including where the pack's quotation is wrong or incomplete. If a provision cannot be retrieved, say so and do not fill the gap from memory.

**Primary text to fetch (current revised versions, as at 21 September 2026).**
- MLR 2017 (SI 2017/692): regs 3(1), 18, 19, 27, 28, 31, 33, 34. legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/692. Also the version of reg 33 as at 29 June 2026.
- SI 2026/621: regs 1, 19, 20 and the Explanatory Note. legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/621/made.
- PSRs 2017 (SI 2017/752): regs 40, 51 to 51D, as inserted or substituted by SI 2025/688. PARs 2015 reg 26 as amended.
- FCA Handbook PRIN 2A.1 and 2A.2. FCTR 12.3.6G to 12.3.8G.
- FATF Guidance on Financial Inclusion and AML/CFT Measures (June 2025), paras 47, 52, 53, 140 to 142.
- JMLSG Part I (Aug 2025) 4.33, 4.57, 4.62, 4.74 to 4.77, 5.2.6 to 5.2.7. Part II (Dec 2025) 16.1, 16.27, 16.28, 16.32 to 16.34.

**Claims to test.** For each, give a verdict from: Supported / Supported with conditions / Not supported / Cannot determine from the text.

1. **Reg 33(1)(b) and the lists.** What did reg 33(3)(a) say before and after 30 June 2026, and what follows for FATF Increased Monitoring jurisdictions as a mandatory EDD trigger? Separately: does the Increased Monitoring list operate in reg 33 only through 33(6)(c), 33(1)(a) or 33(1)(g)? Or does the text say something else? Note that the statute never names that list after the amendment.
2. **Reg 31 and "customer due diligence measures".** Reg 31(1) says "unable to apply customer due diligence measures as required by regulation 28". Reg 3(1) defines the term as "the measures required by regulation 28, and where relevant, those required by regulations 29 and 33 to 37". Does inability to complete reg 33 EDD or reg 34(1) measures engage reg 31? Set out each reading the text allows and the strongest textual argument for each. Say whether any authority (case, FCA, Treasury) resolves it.
3. **Reg 34 scope.** To whom do the 34(1) measures apply, and what does 33(1)(c) ("in accordance with regulation 34") add, if anything, for a respondent that is not from a third country?
4. **PSRs 2017 regs 51B and 51C.** Test: the 28 April 2026 vintage condition and reg 51A; the 51C(a) and (c) exemptions; whether a discretionary AML exit (CDD complete, no suspicion) falls inside 51B; how the 51B(2)(a) reasons duty sits with 51B(4) and the tipping-off offences (POCA s333A, TA s21D); and the reg 40(7) disapplication for non-consumer users. Also whether a correspondent banking arrangement is a payment services framework contract at all.
5. **PRIN 2A and AML exits.** Does PRIN 2A.2.8R, 2.10G(3) or 2.10G(5)(iv) apply to an AML-driven account closure? State what the text says, what it does not say, and whether any FCA statement (for example FG22/5) answers the point. We treat any such application as our own assessment.
6. **JMLSG 16.27.** It says correspondent banking firms "must apply the Enhanced Due Diligence measures in full". Compare with reg 34(1), 34(4) and 33(1)(c). Is the JMLSG "must" broader than the statute?
7. **FATF Guidance para 142.** It says regulated entities are "only required under FATF Standards to terminate or reject customer relationships, on a case-by-case basis, where the ML/TF risks cannot be mitigated". Check this against the Standards themselves (R.10, R.13, INR.10), not only the Guidance. Is it a fair statement of the Standards, and is any UK legal consequence being wrongly implied when read next to reg 31?
8. **Scenario verdict wording (section 10).** We plan two scenarios: (A) a bank respondent in an Increased Monitoring jurisdiction, and (B) a UK small customer, one branch with CDD incomplete and one branch with CDD complete and residual risk high. For each planned outcome, tell us whether describing it as mandatory or discretionary is supported by the primary text, and where we would be presenting inference as law.

**Return format per claim.** Verdict; operative words quoted from the primary text with provision reference; structural check (correct limb, conditions, entity scope, exceptions, effect); what the guide may safely say; what it must not say; any citation or quotation error in the pack.

**Known gaps you should not fill from memory.** JMLSG Sept 2026 and Feb 2026 revisions (unread, awaiting HMT approval). HM Treasury's rationale for the reg 33(1)(b) change (not stated in the SI or Explanatory Note).
