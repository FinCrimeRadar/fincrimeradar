**POLICY PROPOSAL, not yet adopted: Digital Asset Attribution Standard.** Surfaced during stablecoin series scoping (2026-08-28). Proposes a reusable evidence schema for any guide citing blockchain analytics (TRM, Chainalysis, Elliptic, Crystal, or similar providers), covering two distinct claim types: quantitative claims (how much moved, when, through what addresses, according to whom, using what methodology) and attribution claims (who allegedly owns, operates, controls, funds, administers or is associated with assets/addresses, and what authority supports that conclusion).

Proposed evidence object fields for quantitative claims: provider, report_title, publication_date, measurement_period, metric, unit, value, asset_or_entity, attribution_methodology, measurement_type, internal_transfers_included, cross_chain_activity_included, provider_qualification (preserve the provider's own qualifying language, e.g. identified/associated/linked/suspected/attributed/received/exposed/processed, do not flatten these into a single word like "handled"), primary_source_url, verification_status, verified_at.

Proposed four-level attribution classification for entity claims: Level 1 Officially designated (named by OFAC, OFSI, EU, UN or other competent authority), Level 2 Officially attributed (named by a government, regulator, law enforcement agency or court, not necessarily designated), Level 3 Analytics attributed (linked by Chainalysis, TRM, Elliptic or similar), Level 4 Open source associated (media/researcher/academic reporting without stronger attribution). A token, wallet, issuer, individual or related entity must not be described as sanctioned solely because it is associated with sanctioned infrastructure or flagged by an analytics provider; sanctions status must be independently checked against the applicable official list.

Do not implement into CLAUDE.md or verification-ledger.json yet. Validate the schema in practice while sourcing Guide 4 (A7A5), then propose formal adoption as a CLAUDE.md sourcing-standard addition once proven.
